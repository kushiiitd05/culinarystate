**Verdict.**

- **What is sound:**
  - **Cap:** min(T_medium, T_boil) with the same h is a defensible first-order evaporative-boundary model for moist interiors and removes the ~10² dose inflation. My hand checks give realistic cores: a 2.5-cm chicken breast at 180 °C for 30 min reaches about 74 °C at INTENSE; 1-cm dice deep-fried reach 94 °C.
  - **Dry-class exemptions:** right for single-group events. Oil itself, nuts and sugar are uncapped. Food fried in oil stays capped because the matrix is the food's group. Doughs are capped as ambiguous, which is correct for the crumb. Meats are moist and nuts dry.
  - **Pressure cooking:** the 5/10/15 psig bracket of 108.4/115.2/121.0 °C matches steam tables, and clipping to 100 °C on release is right.
  - **Under-estimates:** the capped trajectory is not a bound on reality (frying behaves almost like a fixed 100 °C surface; ovens show a wet-bulb plateau), and it under-reads in the row 2–3 cases. Nothing outside `simulation/` reads Layer-2 doses today, so no live claim is under-stated.
  - **Non-heat formulas:** Plank, the mirrored-Plank thaw, the Crank slab uptake, the two-stage drying model and the exact modal stepper are correct, with consistent units. Only their scope (rows 4–5) is wrong.
  - **Lot state:** the ratio bounds are exact over the mass box with outward rounding. UNKNOWN never becomes 0. The pH flags are diagnostics only: no gate reads them. Water activity is OFF and the Norrish sign is right.
  - **Router:** the gates never raise a route's strength and emit codes only. No SR composition number leaks into an emitted output.
  - **SSOT:** the §285.8 pH bounding rule is a valid theorem under its stated assumptions, and §286–§288 are sound. Only §284.3, §284.4 and §284.5 need rewording.
- **Fit for a NON_CITABLE demo:** yes, once rows 6–7 are fixed. As an interim measure, a HIGH-only ABSENT screen and withholding DIRECT on already-processed lots would do. Row 1 must be fixed before any lot-state census or a_w value is reported.
- **Fit for M3:** the M3 vitamin-C fit itself (Step 10 paper kinetics on acidic fruit liquids) does not read Layer 2 and can proceed. The physical-state dataset is **not** yet fit to drive sim_aa_1 on recipe events until two things are done: zone-resolved local doses that use M3's own Ea/z (row 2), and a solute-aware boiling point for jams and syrups (row 3). The GENTLE–INTENSE bracket should also gain a fixed-100 °C-surface end and a wet-bulb end, so it covers the boundary-condition uncertainty.
