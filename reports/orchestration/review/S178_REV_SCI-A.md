The problems are in three places:
- **Layer 1 temperature ownership** (M1–M3). Food-probe targets and until-clause temperatures are still bound as medium temperatures. Oven and oil temperatures cross into pan and oven steps. A later clause's oven word deletes a liquid's own temperature.
