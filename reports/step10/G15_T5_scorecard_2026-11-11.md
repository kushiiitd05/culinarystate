The definitions below are the **v13 revised Level-15 gates** retained in SSOT v15, section 215.38,

formal validation deliverable; that scope decision does not make the formal scientific gates pass.

Statuses follow the board's requested vocabulary. PARTIAL means some relevant evidence exists but the full
criterion is not established; NEED_DATA means the required observations or evaluation are absent; NOT_MET
means the required demonstration is absent or a necessary condition fails. These are assessed statuses of
the existing criteria, not newly invented numeric thresholds. **No gate below is assessed PASS.**
