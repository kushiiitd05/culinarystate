## Top items (in priority order)

1. **21-7 + 21-8**: pH (and T for non-enzymatic chemistry) coded as NECESSARY windows is chemically wrong. The schema then lets a MODULATING-only entry pass as `gates_unknown=false`, which gives a vacuous APPLICABLE. Fix the rule and the schema branch together.
2. **21-5**: G5 makes SIMULATE unreachable for SIM-AA-1, because the lumped apparent k_d is not representable and ascorbate oxidase is always UNKNOWN. Add a lumped entry plus FORM `covers_mechanism_keys`.
3. **21-1 / 21-2**: split AA → DHA (reversible, O2/metal/ascorbate-oxidase catalysed) from DHA → DKG (hydrolysis, O2-independent). Ascorbate oxidase is a catalysed route of step 1, not a separate loss.
4. **21-3 / 21-4**: no catalyst/promoter construct (metals, light, chelators), and no enzyme-thermal-inactivation entry. Without them, blanching, start mode and the come-up window cannot be evaluated.
5. **21-6 / 21-16**: chlorophyllase and Zn/Cu-complex pathways are missing from the chlorophyll fallback.
6. **21-9**: OPEN_STATED bounds extrapolate to APPLICABLE beyond the studied range.
7. **35-1**: G35-SEP "passes by structure" although STRUCTURAL / CHEMICAL / CULTURAL and STRUCTURAL / SENSORY share evaluation targets.
8. **35-2 / 35-3**: Recipe1M ⊃ food.com contamination beyond the relevance ban; taste labels are positive-unlabelled and confounded by source database.
9. **19-1 / 19-2**: α thresholds applied to F1; the STOP-SCHEMA blocking rule needs convergence and coarse categories, so schema problems will be under-detected.
