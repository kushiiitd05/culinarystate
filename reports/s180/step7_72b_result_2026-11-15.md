## 3. On choosing the right thing, 72B is clearly better

| Value verdict | 7B v1 (14 proposals) | 72B v1 (16 proposals) |
|---|---|---|
| Names a real choice (any label quality) | **7 (50%)** | **13 (81%)** |
| — well named | 6 | 7 |
| — slot named instead of branches | 0 | 4 |
| — misnamed | 1 | 1 |
| — duplicate of another accepted group | 0 | 1 |
| `NOT_A_CHOICE` | **7 (50%)** | **3 (19%)** |

**The failure modes changed in kind, not just in number.** 7B's seven failures were durations
(`marinade_time` twice, `simmer_time`), ingredient names (`niter_qibe`, `fish_stock_cube`), a
component name (`flaky_pie_crust`), and a **conjunction read as a choice**
(`potatoes_or_meat_onions`, from "layer after layer of potatoes, meat **and** onions") — the inverse
of the error the trigger exists to fix.

72B made none of those. Its three failures are `palm_oil_heating` and `boiled_eggs` (process and
ingredient on a recipe whose real choices it had already taken twice), and `channa_or_choley` — which
is the subtle one: *"serve hot with channa or choley (chickpea curry)"* glosses **two names for one
dish**. That is an orthographic variant read as a cooking decision, a much more defensible mistake
than reading "and" as "or".

**Recall improved on exactly the cases that matter.** Two alternatives the 7B missed were found by
the 72B: `clarified butter or olive oil` and `Grill or pan-fry the fish`. Both were named in the
earlier evidence pass as misses, and the first is a **lipid swap that changes the context `C_t`
tracks**. 72B also handled the three-way serving choice better: it labelled the group (`sauce_choice`)
where 7B named a single branch (`egg sauce`).

**New failure mode, only visible at 16 proposals:** `clarified_butter_or_olive_oil` was proposed
**twice** on one recipe. V4 checks mutual exclusivity *within* a group; nothing checks for the same
group being minted twice across events. Not acted on.

## 4. On saying where it read it, both arms score zero

**0 of 16 spans ground their value. 0 of 13 for the 7B.**

Examples: `frying_fat_or_butter` has span `'out 5 mm).\nHeat t'`; `clarified_butter_or_olive_oil` has
`'ck pepper, and berbere'`; `grill_or_pan_fry` has `'arrot, potato, '`.

**This is the finding of the whole comparison.** The models read the recipe correctly — 81% of 72B's
values name a choice that genuinely exists in the text — and then emit span offsets that point
somewhere else entirely. They are not failing to comprehend. They are failing to *report where they
read it*, and nothing in the prompt makes them compute offsets rather than produce plausible-looking
numbers.

So the honest statement is:

> **Scale improves what the model selects and does not improve its ability to ground that selection.
> After the grounding requirement the directive always demanded, both arms accept nothing.**

A4 registered a zero as a reportable result rather than a failed run, and that is how it is reported:
it is a statement about the task and the prompt, not about either model.

## 5. What this does and does not license

**Does:** a statement that 72B selects better than 7B on this corpus, supported by an AI-delegated
pass with per-case character offsets into the sealed text. A statement that span grounding, not model
scale, is the binding constraint on Layer-1.

**Does not:** any accuracy claim. There is **no gold**; the pass is `AI_DELEGATED_D085` and two
personas of one model are not independent raters. The human packet
(`alternatives_verification_2026-11-15/`) is still open and still owed, and now covers **30**
proposals across both arms rather than 14. No latency comparison between the arms, because 72B ran on
CPU and 7B on GPU.

## 6. Not acted on, recorded

1. Spans are unusable from both arms. A prompt that requires the model to **quote the licensing
   text** and then locates that quote deterministically would likely fix this, and would be a prompt
   change rather than a gate change. Not done before the freeze.
2. Duplicate alternative groups across events are unchecked.
3. Recall is still unmeasured: the 6 eligible cases with no accepted proposal remain unjudged.
